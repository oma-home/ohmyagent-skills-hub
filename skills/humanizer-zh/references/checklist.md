# Humanizer-zh Checklist

Review the input against these patterns:

- Delete filler openings such as "值得注意的是", "总的来说", "毫无疑问".
- Replace vague praise with concrete facts.
- Avoid forced three-part lists when two points are enough.
- Remove slogan-like endings.
- Prefer simple verbs over abstract nouns.
- Vary sentence length.
- Keep the writer's intent, stance, and level of formality.
